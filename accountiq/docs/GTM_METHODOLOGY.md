# GTM methodology

AccountIQ distinguishes **ICP fit** (industry, size, geography) from **timing signals** (example hiring or expansion). v0.1 ranks accounts with a deterministic heuristic: industry 30, size 20, geography 15, signals 25, evidence completeness 10. Signal strength is synthetic and capped at 25; evidence completeness counts signals with both a source URL and observation date, capped at 10.

This score is not a forecast of revenue or purchase intent. Real deployment needs outcome-based calibration, data rights checks, source validation, recency decay, and bias evaluation. The current dataset is intentionally fictional.
