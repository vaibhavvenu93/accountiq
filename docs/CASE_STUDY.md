# Demo case study: founder-led outbound

**Scenario (fictional):** A B2B SaaS founder has 12 mixed-industry accounts and wants to focus on UK/EU companies with 10–500 employees. AccountIQ filters by fit, scores illustrative signals, and displays the scoring breakdown. The founder can change the industry or region and rerun the ranking.

**What this proves:** repeatable, inspectable ranking and separation of scoring from UI.

**What it does not prove:** improved conversion, validated buying intent, or access to real market intelligence. No customer outcomes are claimed.
